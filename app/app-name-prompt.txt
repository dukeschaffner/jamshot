About
This is an app/website that allows music artists to collaborate with smaller artists/fans, but in more of a social media format. for instance, an artist would upload a clip that they recorded, and people could attach audio files onto the original recording as a new version. the app would have a like/ranking system and have a featured page to show popular collabs.


Goals/VALUE
- music collaboration as a social media
- Democratize music creation
- allow musicians to make a meaningful/valuable piece of work without having to complete the process of recording a song from start to finish
- tracks will be capped at 1:30 to make it simple, fun, and pair well with short form video content like Tik Tok.
- The equivalent of going from books/publications to posts on social media. New content is released daily and from more sources, and just as social media posts allow you to comment your textual thoughts/ideas on someone else's textual posts, with this app, you can comment your musical thoughts/ideas on someone else's musical posts
- The goal is to encourage artists to share as much as possible, not feeling hindered by the desire or pressure to completely finish/polish a song, or have just the right name or album artwork, opening the musical floodgates.

Features
- home feed with tracks/collabs posted by user's follows mixed with popular tracks
  - click on a track to expand and see other collabs
- users will have profiles and can be verified artists. There will be a profile page with the user's tracks, picture, reposted tracks, liked tracks, follower count, etc
- It will also work as a streaming service, were a user can pick a genre and just listen
  - tracks will have genre and instrument tags to help accommodate this
- strong interaction between artists/fans or smaller artists, as well as friend groups who want to quickly and easily share ideas. success likely determined by how many medium to large artist adopt and use the platform.
