

🎧 For Best Results (Low Latency Recording)

To get the smoothest, most responsive recording experience:

✅ Use Headphones

Avoid using your device speakers.
Headphones prevent echo and reduce delay.

🎤 Use an External Mic or Audio Interface (Recommended)

USB microphones, headsets, or audio interfaces perform much better than built-in laptop mics.

🌐 Use Chrome or Edge

For the lowest latency and best compatibility, we recommend:

Google Chrome
Microsoft Edge

(Firefox and Safari may have higher delay on some setups)

🔇 Disable System Audio Effects

If possible, turn off:

Noise suppression
Echo cancellation
Auto gain control

These can add extra delay.

⚡ Close Other Apps

Apps using your microphone or audio (Zoom, Discord, etc.) can increase latency.

🎛️ Lower Your Buffer Size (If Using External Interface)

If your device/software allows it, use a lower buffer size for better responsiveness.

💡 Pro Tip

For near-zero latency recording, you can:

Use your own DAW (Ableton, Logic, FL Studio, etc.)
Then upload your recording here
