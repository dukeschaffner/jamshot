Competition feature:
- users can host a competition around their track
- competition has cash prize
- browse competitions on competition page
- pinned competitions have higher viz on competition page
- sponsored comp pinned on home page and top of competition page
- comp host decides winner selection method (curated, likes/plays, hybrid)
- gifted competitions - provide a voucher for a free partially preconfigured 
- private competitions - only access via code

Two main types of competitions:

1. User-Hosted Competitions

Flow:

A user pays a fee to host a competition around their track.

Example: $50 entry → $35 goes into the prize pool, $15 goes to the platform.

Winner is picked either by:
Host (curated)
Votes/likes (community-driven)
Hybrid (top 5 by votes, host chooses winner).

Upsells:
“Pin” option → higher tier pricing, guarantees visibility on the home/featured page.

2. Sponsored Competitions
Partner with plugins, sample companies, DAWs, audio gear brands.
competition shown on home page and pinned at top of competition page
competition display is branded with company logo and name
Company pays a flat sponsorship fee ($500–$5,000+).
Prize money OR winner gets software or gear


🔒 Subscription Tie-Ins

Here’s where you can bake competitions into the tiers:

Free
Can join competitions. 
Cannot host competitions.

Basic
Can host competitions.
Can host 1 free competition per month (with small prize pool, $5–10).

Premium
Discounted competition hosting fees.
Get 1 free pinned competition per year or discounted pinned fee.
OR
$15 credit to hosting/pinned competition fees
Early access to sponsored competitions (exclusivity feels big).
