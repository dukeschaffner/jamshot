# Competition feature:
- users can host a competition around their track
- competition has cash prize ($5 minimum)
- can be 1 day to 1 month long
- browse competitions on competition page
- pinned competitions have higher viz on competition page (for a fee of $25)
- sponsored comp pinned on home page and top of competition page
- comp host decides winner selection method (curated, automated)
    - curated - host picks the winner (winner automatically chosen if 24 hrs passes)
    - automated - winner is the track with the most likes (tiebreaker: play count, then random choice)
- gifted/preconfigured competitions - provide a voucher for a free partially preconfigured. this will help get bigger artists to host competitions
- one entry per user (always free)
- host cannot enter themselves
- competition tracks still show up in other pages (search, user profile, home feed, etc) but with a banner indicating they are a competition track (if competition is active)
- winner gets notification/email + stripe payout
- track cannot be a layer 5 track ( if must be able to allow more collabs)
- track cannot be deleted while competition is active if there are any entries
- any track uploaded to the competition track during the competition will be automatically entered into the competition
- host can cancel competition up until it has it's first entry. cancel options: delete track AND competitino or just the competition
- link to contact page from competition page for help/support
- cost to host competition is prize_pool + prize_pool * 0.15 (15% platform fee)


## Two main types of competitions:

1. User-Hosted Competitions
A user pays a fee to host a competition around their track.
Upsells:
“Pin” option → higher tier pricing, guarantees visibility on the home/featured page.

2. Sponsored Competitions
Partner with plugins, sample companies, DAWs, audio gear brands.
competition shown on home page and pinned at top of competition page
competition display is branded with company logo and name
Company pays a flat sponsorship fee ($500–$5,000+).
Prize money OR winner gets software or gear


## Subscription Tie-Ins

Free
Can join competitions. 
Cannot host competitions.

Basic
Can host competitions.

Premium
No hosting fees.
Get 1 free pinned competition per year.
