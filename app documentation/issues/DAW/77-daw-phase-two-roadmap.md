Umbrella for advanced DAW work:

- Optional “advanced” or “extension” editor: simple layer DAW by default, user can opt into advanced mode.
- Two collab types: layers vs extensions (define semantics and UX).
- Evaluate paid tiers for advanced DAW / extension collabs.
- Let track owners restrict which collab types are allowed on a track.
- Solid undo/redo in the advanced surface (may overlap with existing DAW undo work).
