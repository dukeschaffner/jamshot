

motives:
1)
connecting artists in a way that is music/inspiration centered instead of project centered
- I played, wrote and recorded music with my brother and our friend throughout high school and college. after college, we all went our separate ways and stopped making music together. we did try to stay connected musically using tools like online collaborative DAWs, but I'd create a project and get everything set up, but then we'd be starting from scratch every time. no starter ideas, no inspiration. and between that annd just having a hard time getting schedules to align, the projects never amounted to anything. we needed something that felt more like jamming.

i created sterio as a way to collaborate with people across the globe in a way that is music/inspiration centered instead of project centered. My vision for the app is for people to post daily updates of their ideas and add their own takes on other people's ideas.



2)
collaborate with anyone, anytime, anywhere
as an indie artist, I always dreamed of collaborating with my favorite artists, but getting your chance to prove yourself was difficult and rare. and these artists are just too busy to work with every indie artist who reaches out.
- i created sterio to allow indie artists to "shoot their shot" in a meaningful way without requiring tons of effort from larger artists. anyone can add their take - the best ones rise to the top and get the recognition they deserve.

3)
open source music
- in my opinion, the best things in life are open source. everybody contributes their own skills and ideas to make something the best it can be. my goal with sterio is to apply this principle to music creation. artists post their ideas and other artists can add their own takes and inspiration on it.


4)
putting the fun back into music creation
- writing music gave me so much joy and I wanted to share my works with others, so i started releasing my songs as an indie artist. but it got to where it was a grind and i was focusing more on trying to get my music to perform as good as possible instead of just making music for the fun of it. then I realized I didn't really enjoy the release process - creating an identity for EVERY release like album artwork, videos, etc. I was also finding much more joy in getting my initial ideas recorded than working to create a finished, polished product every time. I just wanted to make music. i ended up with a hard drive full of unfinished song demos and ideas just collecting dust. 
- with sterio, those demos can now be used to expand their audience and get new inspiration from other artists' contributions without any pressure to create a full identity for the track. no album artwork, no videos, just the music.


5)
a chance for everyone
i believe that all musicians should have a chance to establish themselves and prove themselves regardless of their level skill or connections/popularity. if someone writes great basslines, but they can't sing or play enough instruments to make a full song, and if they don't have inspiration or skill to make videos/album artwork, etc., they should still be able to get their work heard and appreciated. 
- i created sterio as a way to give everyone a chance to make a name for themselves as artists. artists contribute what they can and other artists with complementary skills can add their own takes and inspiration on it to fill in the gaps.
