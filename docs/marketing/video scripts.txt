vid 2 - lowering the barrier to entry:
One of my goals when creating stereo was to lower the barrier to entry to creating and sharing music. there are several features that support that goal.
Let’s say I don’t have a DAW or or I Only know how to play guitar and sing. Normally, I wouldn;t really have a chance of turning recordings into a full song. now with stereo I can just upload my track even though it’s just guitar and vocals, and then other musicians or artists can add to my track to make it a more complete song. Also, there is no album artwork in the app, so you will never have the excuse of not having an album artwork to prevent you from sharing your music with the world. My vision is that instead of waiting months or years to release fully polished songs, or not releasing songs at all because they are not fully polished, you can share your work with the world on a daily basis as you create and get feedback and collaborate with other artists to make it better.

Length: ~45 seconds
🎙️ Making music shouldn’t require a studio, expensive software, or a full band.
That’s why I created STERiO — to make it easy for anyone to create and share their music.
Let’s say you only play guitar or sing — you can upload your track, and other musicians can add drums, bass, or anything else to bring it to life.
There’s no album artwork, no pressure, no waiting months for a “perfect” release.
Just record, upload, and connect with other artists instantly.
Don’t wait for perfect — share your sound today on STERiO.
